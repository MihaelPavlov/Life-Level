More than wings. Each card is one treatment module — a back-prop, a ground, an aura, a sky beam, a companion — done richer (gradient fills, blur, rim-light, contact shadow, vignette). A rank stacks 2–4 of them; the mix is also class-themed (Runner → storm + wind trails, Strength → molten ground + ember wings, Yoga → arcane circle + petals). The hero PNG never changes.
