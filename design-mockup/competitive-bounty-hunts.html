Competitive Engagement Concept #4

Bounty Hunts — one winner, real urgency

Where Thrones and Trials are always-on, Bounties are events: the Realm posts an objective — "first solo Frost Wyrm kill," "first to 100 km of Yoga this season" — everyone racing sees everyone else's progress, and only one hunter claims it. Losing racers still bank a consolation reward.

A different feeling on purpose
Thrones (persistent) and Trials (solo, evergreen) are low-pressure. Bounties are scarce and time-boxed — the one format designed to create FOMO and urgency, deliberately, in small doses.
Reuses what's built
A bounty is an Achievement condition (ConditionType/TargetValue) with a countdown and a single-claim rule, spotlighting a Boss, zone, or stat that's otherwise idle content.
Free marketing content, generated not authored
Every claim is a ready-made "Boss-fight highlight reel" moment from the Marketing doc's content pillars — the Realm Feed is the raw footage, with zero extra production cost.
