Competitive Engagement Concept #3

Ghost Trials — race your own splits, not just your totals

A third axis: pace, not volume or mastery. Every zone path and dungeon corridor already has a distance — Ghost Trials turns your logged pace on it into a replay you can race against your own PB, a friend, or the fastest player in the game. No new art, no new zones.

Fixes the #1 risk in the room
Marketing's audit names content production cost as the category's actual killer — players outpace what a small team can author. Trials need zero new content: every existing path is already a course.
Reuses what's built
A trial is just an Activity's duration/distance re-projected onto a MapEdge/dungeon corridor's checkpoints. No new backend math — a replay layer over data already logged.
A different player, not the same one
Thrones and Sieges reward volume. Trials reward pace — pulls in Strava's plateaued runners who already obsess over splits, not just distance.
