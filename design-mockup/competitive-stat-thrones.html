Competitive Engagement Concept #2

Thrones — five crowns, always up for grabs

A different axis entirely: no teams, no matchmaking, no time window. Each of the five stats has one Monarch — whoever has trained it the hardest, lifetime. Anyone, anytime, can dethrone them simply by surpassing their number. Persistent prestige instead of a scheduled event.

Stats cap at 100 — so what's the number?
Stats stop at 100, so a raw-value throne would end in a permanent tie. Thrones rank by Mastery — an uncapped lifetime ledger of every stat-XP gain, the same numbers already fired by ActivityLoggedEvent, just never capped.
Reuses what's built
No new game math: Mastery is a running sum of existing stat-gain events. The crown badge and equippable "Monarch of X" title reuse the exact Title/Rank display pattern already on Profile.
Reachable at every level
Global throne is for veterans. Friends and Guild scopes give a level-8 player a crown worth chasing on day one — same cold-start fix as before, different shape.
