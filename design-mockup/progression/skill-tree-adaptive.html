Adaptive Skill Tree

A behavior-driven progression system for Life-Level. The class root gives identity, real workouts reveal branches, and player choices convert that proof into map, boss, quest, and streak perks.

Step 1
Start from class identity, not a giant generic tree.
Step 2
Reveal branches from real training patterns and engagement habits.
Step 3
Let players choose among earned nodes that change gameplay loops.
Step 4
Hide mastery paths until the game can say who the player is becoming.

Retention Value
Mastery reveal is where the tree becomes memorable. The player is no longer selecting a perk. They are receiving a title for their behavior.
Recommended build order: start with 4 class roots, 4 universal branches, and a small number of strong nodes tied to map travel, streaks, bosses, and quests. Keep the first release narrow and readable. The hidden mastery reveal is the differentiator.
