Life-Level Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

Life-Level turns real-world activity into in-app RPG progress. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

When you use Life-Level, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: username, email address, password hash, login state, and account identifiers.
  • App progress data: character progress, XP, levels, quests, streaks, rewards, items, achievements, titles, and activity history.
  • Activity and health data you authorize: workout sessions, activity type, duration, distance, calories, steps, and related activity metadata from Health Connect or connected fitness services.
  • Integration data: connection status and tokens for supported services such as Strava, Garmin, or Health Connect, when you choose to connect them.
  • Device notification data: push notification tokens used to send app-related notifications, if notifications are enabled.
  • Technical data: basic app, server, and diagnostic information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your Life-Level account.
  • Convert workouts and activity into XP, quests, streaks, map progress, rewards, and character progression.
  • Show your activity history, achievements, titles, and in-app progress.
  • Sync activity from user-authorized health and fitness integrations.
  • Send app-related notifications, such as progress or streak reminders, when enabled.
  • Protect the app, prevent abuse, debug issues, and improve reliability.

Life-Level does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Health and activity data is used only for user-facing app progress and gameplay features.

Health Connect and Fitness Data

Life-Level only accesses Health Connect or fitness service data after you grant permission. You can revoke those permissions at any time in Android Health Connect settings or in the connected service settings.

We do not sell health or fitness data. We do not use health or fitness data for advertising.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data only with service providers needed to operate Life-Level, such as hosting, database, authentication, analytics, crash diagnostics, push notification, or fitness integration providers. These providers process data only as needed to support the app.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect users, or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

Data Retention

We keep account, progress, and activity data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

Your Choices

  • You can choose whether to connect Health Connect or third-party fitness services.
  • You can revoke Health Connect permissions in Android system settings.
  • You can disconnect supported integrations inside the app where available.
  • You can disable push notifications in your device settings.
  • You can request account deletion by contacting us.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data. No system is completely secure, but we work to reduce risk and protect the information needed to operate Life-Level.

Children

Life-Level is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate consent, we will take steps to delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
